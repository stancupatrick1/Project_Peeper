01 / Personality

Not just a screen.

PEEPER turns movement into expression. Give it a shake, tilt it around, or leave it alone for a little while.

01 — REACT

Eyes that follow motion

Googly pupils slosh around as you move the keychain. The accelerometer gives the eyes a sense of direction and momentum.

02 — PLAY

More than one look

Switch between eye styles, change colours, and discover different expressions using the MODE button.

03 — REST

Knows when to nap

After a period without movement, the eyes get sleepy, close, and the ESP32-S3 enters deep sleep until motion wakes it.

05 / Get started

Bring it to life.

The project is designed to be inspectable and modifiable. Start with the source project and documentation.

Fabricate the PCB

Upload the Gerber ZIP to your PCB manufacturer. Use the BOM and CPL files if you are ordering assembly.

Set up the firmware

Install Arduino IDE, the Espressif ESP32 boards package, and LovyanGFX. Open the PEEPER firmware project.

Flash and explore

Configure the ESP32-S3 board settings according to the project documentation, then explore the eye modes.

Check battery protection, polarity, and assembly before powering your board.
